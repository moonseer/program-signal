# Platform Signal — Content Rights Policy

**Status:** Editorial operating policy  
**Public summary:** linked from [`/editorial-standards`](/editorial-standards)

Prefer original work. Attribute everything that is not ours.

---

## Third-party asset metadata

When a third-party asset is committed (image, excerpted figure, audio, video still), record at minimum:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `source` | Origin URL or archive reference |
| `creator` | Author or rights holder if known |
| `license` | SPDX id or plain-language license |
| `permission_basis` | license, fair use assessment, written permission, public domain |
| `attribution` | Required credit string |
| `used_in` | Article id(s) or path(s) |

Store metadata next to the asset or in the article package references when practical.

---

## Diagrams

- Prefer **original** Platform Signal diagrams (`PS-D-NNNN`).
- Do not paste vendor architecture diagrams as our own.
- Redrawn diagrams inspired by a public figure still need attribution when the structure is distinctive and not independently standard.

---

## Code

- Short illustrative snippets may appear in articles when needed for operator clarity.
- Respect upstream licenses. Do not paste substantial GPL (or similarly conditioned) code into All Rights Reserved editorial prose without a license-compatible path.
- Prefer linking to official docs for long manifests.

Site software remains under the repository’s MIT license for code. Editorial content remains all rights reserved unless a file says otherwise.

---

## Research figures

- Do not scrape paywalled figures into the corpus.
- Summarize findings in prose and cite the paper.
- If a figure is essential and license permits, keep provenance metadata.

---

## AI-generated assets

When AI assists a diagram, illustration, or similar asset:

- Treat it as a Platform Signal work product under editorial accountability
- Do not claim photographic or documentary authenticity
- Do not train readers to believe an AI render is a real production screenshot unless it is one
- Keep the same evidence rules: the diagram must not invent systems the sources do not support
