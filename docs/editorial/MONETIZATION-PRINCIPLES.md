# Platform Signal — Monetization Principles

**Status:** Policy only (no active monetization)  
**Companion:** [`SPONSORSHIP-POLICY.md`](./SPONSORSHIP-POLICY.md)

These principles describe what may be considered later. None are active on Vercel Hobby.

---

## Hard gate

No monetization until:

1. The site runs on a **commercial-capable host** (for example Vercel Pro), and
2. **Legal review** of the chosen model is complete, and
3. Disclosure UI and frontmatter gates are in place.

---

## Allowed future models (candidates)

| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| Newsletter sponsorship | Clearly labeled; sponsor does not buy conclusions |
| Site sponsorship | Persistent disclosure; no sold rankings |
| Affiliate links | Labeled; no stealth steering of “best” picks |
| Workshops / training | Separate from editorial recommendations |
| Premium research | Paid depth without rewriting free conclusions |
| Job board | Labeled listings; not editorial endorsements |

---

## FTC-style disclosure examples (on file)

Use language of this form when a model is active:

- “This newsletter edition is sponsored by Acme. Acme did not write or approve the editorial conclusions.”
- “This article contains affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you buy through them. Affiliates do not change our recommendations.”
- “Acme provided a time-limited evaluation license. Access was disclosed; conclusions remain independent.”

---

## Explicit non-goals

- Selling Adopt/Trial/Hold placements
- Undisclosed native advertising
- Pay-for-PASS evidence review
- Monetizing while remaining on Hobby hosting
