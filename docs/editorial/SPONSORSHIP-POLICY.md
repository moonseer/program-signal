# Platform Signal — Sponsorship Policy

**Status:** Public editorial policy  
**Public page:** [`/sponsorship-policy`](/sponsorship-policy)  
**Companion:** [`EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md`](./EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) §5 and §7

Editorial conclusions cannot be purchased.

---

## Current commercial posture

Platform Signal is hosted on **Vercel Hobby**. Hobby terms do not allow commercial use of the project for paid sponsorships, affiliates, or other monetization.

Until the site runs on a commercial-capable host (for example Vercel Pro) **and** legal review is complete:

- No paid sponsorships
- No affiliate programs
- No sold rankings, packages, or “guaranteed coverage”

This policy exists so the rules are public before revenue is possible. See also [`MONETIZATION-PRINCIPLES.md`](./MONETIZATION-PRINCIPLES.md).

---

## Non-negotiables

These remain true after any future monetization:

1. **No sold rankings.** We do not sell placement in “best of,” Adopt/Trial/Assess-style lists, or comparative tables.
2. **No hidden native ads.** Sponsored material is labeled as sponsored in the UI and in frontmatter.
3. **No vendor-written “independent” analysis.** Vendors may not author copy that Platform Signal publishes as independent editorial.
4. **No paying for a conclusion.** Money cannot buy a PASS, a recommendation, or a softened risk assessment.
5. **Frontmatter must match reality.** `sponsorship.sponsored` and `sponsorship.affiliateLinks` must be accurate. Sponsored content without disclosure fields fails CI.

---

## What may be allowed later (with disclosure)

When commercial hosting and legal review are in place, future options may include clearly labeled newsletter sponsors, site sponsors, affiliate links, workshops, or premium research. Each requires FTC-style disclosure. None of those options are active today.

---

## Vendor access is not sponsorship

Briefings, demos, time-limited licenses, documentation access, and interviews are covered by [`VENDOR-INTERACTION-POLICY.md`](./VENDOR-INTERACTION-POLICY.md). They are not sponsorship unless money or ongoing promotional obligation is involved.
