# Platform Signal — Vendor Interaction Policy

**Status:** Editorial operating policy  
**Public summary:** linked from [`/sponsorship-policy`](/sponsorship-policy) and [`/editorial-standards`](/editorial-standards)  
**Companion:** [`EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md`](./EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) §5

Vendors are sources, not clients.

---

## Allowed (with disclosure when material)

- Product briefings and demos
- Time-limited evaluation licenses
- Official documentation and maintainer interviews
- Clarifying questions after publication about factual statements

Disclose vendor access when it could reasonably affect a reader’s assessment of independence (for example, exclusive early access that shaped the piece).

---

## Forbidden

- Promised coverage, tone, or conclusion in exchange for access
- Full pre-publication editorial approval by a vendor
- Paying Platform Signal for a recommendation or ranking
- Undisclosed gifts intended to influence coverage
- Vendor-authored drafts published as independent Platform Signal analysis

---

## Factual check vs editorial review

| Kind | Allowed? | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Factual check** | Yes | Confirm versions, command names, API shapes, dates, and quoted documentation |
| **Editorial review** | No | Vendors do not approve thesis, framing, risk language, or whether to publish |

A factual check may catch an incorrect flag name. It may not soften “do not use this in production.”

---

## Gifts

Safest default: **do not accept material gifts.**

If a low-value conference item or meal incidental to a briefing occurs, disclose it when covering that vendor in a related piece, or decline. Cash, hardware of material value, travel, and equity are refused.

---

## After publication

Vendors may request corrections through the [`CORRECTIONS-POLICY.md`](./CORRECTIONS-POLICY.md) path. Disagreement with analysis is not an error.
